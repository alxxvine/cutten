Dragon boneling

Something small woke up

A little dragon of bone and emberlight, alone on a dead island. They do not know anyone yet. What is their name?

Mouse or finger, both work. Nothing here can be lost — they are always glad you came.

What to do here

  • Pet them. Drag across the bones — the ember in their chest flares and they lean into your hand.
  • Throw a wisp. Press Wisp, then tap the ash: they will chase it down and carry it back.
  • Feed them. Press Marrow and hold it up to their jaws.
  • Let them rest. When tired they curl up by the bone circle and the dark deepens.
  • Look around. Drag the ground to circle the island, scroll or pinch to come closer.

The longer you stay, the brighter their ember burns — and the more they grow. A grown dragon takes to the air.
