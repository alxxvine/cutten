Your dragon just hatched

They are tiny and do not know anyone yet. What is their name?

Works with a mouse and with a finger. You cannot lose — your dragon is always happy to see you.

What to do here

  • ✋ Pet them. Drag your finger or mouse across the dragon — they squint, lean in and rumble.
  • 🎾 Play fetch. Press “Ball”, then tap the grass: they dash off, grab it and bring it back.
  • 🍓 Feed them. Press “Treat” and hold the berry up to their snout.
  • 💤 Tuck them in. When tired they yawn and curl up on their own. Or press “Sleep”.
  • 🖱 Look around. Drag across the grass to walk around the meadow, scroll or pinch to zoom.

The more time you spend together, the more attached your dragon gets — and the more they grow. A grown dragon learns to fly.
